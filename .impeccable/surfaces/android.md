# Android product surface

Mode: Operate. Extend the existing Saathi paper/forest community noticeboard into native Material 3 controls, without replacing the web identity. Kotlin/Compose, API 26+, phone and tablet; light/dark follow Android.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A readable community noticeboard that keeps relief quantities and saved-work status understandable in the field.

OWN-WORLD: Warm paper, forest ink, restrained green surfaces; local Lora headings and Manrope controls; outlined need cards and familiar native navigation.

STORY: Find a verified need, check freshness, contribute or prepare team work, then distinguish saving, sharing and publication.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Saathi masthead and team action, explicit QA environment and live capability status, existing two-line community invitation, searchable needs; bottom bar becomes a tablet rail.

FORM: Code-led extension of the existing product; no replacement world or image comp. Signature interaction: saved signed updates retain their authorship while changing delivery state.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
